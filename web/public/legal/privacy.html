MSP Hub · Coro Billing

Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

This policy describes how MSP Hub, LLC (“MSP Hub”, “we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Coro Billing workbench at coro-billing.vercel.app (the “Service”), an internal billing tool used by MSP Hub accounting staff and authorized collaborators. The short version: the Service was built to keep your data on your machine — billing files are processed entirely in your browser, and our servers store nothing about your billing close.

1. Billing files stay in your browser

The usage reports, vendor invoices, and pricing sheets you load into the Service are read and processed locally by your browser. They are never uploaded to, or stored on, our servers. Draft invoices, review and approval state, and related working data are kept in your browser’s local storage on your device and never leave it.

2. QuickBooks Online connection

You can optionally connect the Service to QuickBooks Online through Intuit’s OAuth 2.0 authorization flow.

  • The access and refresh tokens Intuit issues are stored only in your browser’s local storage — per browser, per device. Our serverless endpoints relay the authorization and token-refresh exchange to Intuit and pass tokens back to your browser without retaining them.
  • During sign-in, a short-lived (10-minute) HttpOnly cookie holds a random value used solely to protect the OAuth flow against cross-site request forgery. It is not used for tracking.
  • When you push approved invoices, the Service uses your tokens to create invoices — and, if needed, customer records — in the QuickBooks company file you connected, and to look up existing invoices so re-pushes never create duplicates. It reads and writes nothing else in your company file, and it never updates, voids, or deletes existing QuickBooks records.

Data you send to QuickBooks is handled by Intuit under Intuit’s privacy statement.

3. What our servers store

Nothing. The Service has no user accounts and no database. Its serverless functions are stateless: they hold only the application’s own Intuit credentials (as deployment configuration) and process each request without persisting your data.

4. Service providers

  • Vercel hosts the site and serverless functions (United States). Like any host, Vercel may generate standard operational logs (such as IP address and request metadata) under Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the Service’s typefaces; your browser requests font files from Google when a page loads.
  • Intuit processes the QuickBooks authorization and invoice requests described above.

The Service uses no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.

5. Retention and deletion

Because we store nothing server-side, deletion is in your hands: clearing the site’s data in your browser removes all working data, tokens, and pushed-invoice state from your device. You can revoke the Service’s QuickBooks access at any time from your Intuit account’s connected-apps settings, which invalidates its tokens. Invoices already created in QuickBooks remain in your company file under your control.

6. Security

All traffic to the Service uses HTTPS. OAuth state is protected with an HttpOnly cookie, tokens are confined to your browser, and the serverless endpoints validate requests and hold no persistent state. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but the Service’s design minimizes what there is to protect: your billing data simply never reaches us.

7. Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
